Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) and the Nagoya Protocol on Access and Benefit-sharing - overview.
The CBD recognises the sovereign right of States over their biological resources. The Nagoya Protocol provides an international framework for access to genetic resources and for the fair and equitable sharing of the benefits arising from their utilisation (Access and Benefit-sharing, or ABS).
Access to genetic resources generally requires the Prior Informed Consent (PIC) of the providing country and Mutually Agreed Terms (MAT). India gives effect to these obligations domestically through the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 and the National Biodiversity Authority.
A user of Indian biological resources or associated traditional knowledge for research or commercial purposes may therefore face both India's ABS requirements and the ABS requirements of other countries that are parties to the Nagoya Protocol.
