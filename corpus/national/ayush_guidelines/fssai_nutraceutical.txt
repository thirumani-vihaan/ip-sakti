Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 - Section 22, read with the FSS (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals, Food for Special Dietary Use, Food for Special Medical Purpose, Functional Food and Novel Food) Regulations, 2016.
Nutraceuticals, health supplements and foods for special dietary or medical use are regulated as food by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), not as drugs. They may contain plants or botanicals, vitamins, minerals, amino acids or other permitted ingredients within specified limits.
Such products must meet labelling, permitted-ingredient and claims requirements, and must not claim to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
An Ayurvedic ingredient marketed as a food or health supplement generally falls under FSSAI, whereas the same ingredient marketed with therapeutic ASU claims falls under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940. Correct classification determines which licence and authority apply.
