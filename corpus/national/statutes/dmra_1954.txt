Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954 - Sections 3 and 4.
Section 3 prohibits the advertisement of any drug in a manner suggesting that it can procure miscarriage or prevent conception, improve or maintain sexual pleasure or capacity, correct menstrual disorders, or diagnose, cure, mitigate, treat or prevent any disease, disorder or condition specified in the Schedule to the Act.
Section 4 prohibits misleading advertisements relating to drugs that make a false claim, or give a false impression about the true character, of the drug, or are otherwise likely to deceive.
These restrictions apply to advertisements of Ayurvedic and other ASU products; a marketing claim that a product cures a disease listed in the Schedule is not permitted.
