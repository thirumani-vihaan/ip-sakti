Biological Diversity Act, 2002 - Section 3.
No person referred to in sub-section (2) shall, without the previous approval of the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA), obtain any biological resource occurring in India or knowledge associated with it for research, for commercial utilisation, or for bio-survey and bio-utilisation.
The persons covered by sub-section (2) are: a person who is not a citizen of India; a citizen of India who is a non-resident; and a body corporate, association or organisation that is either not incorporated or registered in India, or is incorporated or registered in India but has non-Indian participation in its share capital or management.
Such foreign or foreign-controlled applicants must therefore obtain prior NBA approval, whereas Indian entities give prior intimation to the State Biodiversity Board under Section 7.
