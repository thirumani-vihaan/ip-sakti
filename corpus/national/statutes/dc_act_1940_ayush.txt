Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 - Chapter IVA (Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani drugs).
No person shall manufacture for sale or for distribution any Ayurvedic, Siddha or Unani (ASU) drug except under, and in accordance with the conditions of, a licence issued by the State Licensing Authority (Section 33EEC).
ASU drugs must not be misbranded, adulterated or spurious (Sections 33EE, 33EEA and 33EEB). Under the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, "classical" ASU medicines manufactured strictly in accordance with the formulae described in the authoritative books listed in the First Schedule to the Act are regulated differently from "proprietary" ASU medicines, which carry a proprietary name and must provide additional label, ingredient and safety information.
Manufacture of ASU drugs must comply with Good Manufacturing Practices prescribed under Schedule T.
