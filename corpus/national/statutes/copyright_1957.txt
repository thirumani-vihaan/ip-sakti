Copyright Act, 1957 - Sections 13 and 17.
Copyright subsists in original literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works, cinematograph films and sound recordings. Copyright protects the expression of an idea, not the idea itself, and arises automatically on creation; registration is not mandatory.
First owner of copyright (Section 17): the author of a work is generally the first owner of the copyright, subject to exceptions such as works made in the course of the author's employment under a contract of service, where the employer is the first owner in the absence of agreement to the contrary.
Copyright can protect the text of a book, brochure or website describing an Ayurvedic product, but it does not protect the underlying formulation, method or process; those may fall under patent or trade-secret protection.
