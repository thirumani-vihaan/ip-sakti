Designs Act, 2000 - Sections 2(d) and 4.
"Design" means only the features of shape, configuration, pattern, ornament or composition of lines or colours applied to any article in two or three dimensional form by any industrial process, which in the finished article appeal to and are judged solely by the eye. It does not include any mode or principle of construction, anything which is in substance a mere mechanical device, or any trade mark or artistic work.
Prohibition of registration of certain designs (Section 4): a design shall not be registered if it is not new or original, or has been disclosed to the public anywhere in India or in any other country by publication or by use before the filing date, or is not significantly distinguishable from known designs or combinations of known designs, or comprises or contains scandalous or obscene matter.
The visual shape of a distinctive Ayurvedic product container or packaging may be protectable as a registered design if it is new and original.
